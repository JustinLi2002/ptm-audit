# Row-B label: post-result interpretive wording correction (2026-11-03)

PREREG_mouse.md fixed both the condition and the label of every decision row, and it required the label of the observed row to be reported. Its text is kept unchanged.

**Observed row:** B. The TU interval lies below zero and the UU interval above zero. Row A does not hold, because the TT interval includes zero: +0.0096 (−0.0172, +0.0332).

**Label as fixed:** "Training-construction effect replicated; the threshold-sampled test does not hide it." It is reported as fixed, in Supplementary Note 9.

**Correction, made after the results were known:**
- The first clause describes the result.
- The second clause can be read as saying that the threshold-sampled test reveals the harm. It does not: TT shows neither a clear benefit, as in all eight human tasks, nor the harm seen on the unrestricted test.
- The result is therefore described as a replication of the training-construction effect on an independent source, in which a benefit on threshold-sampled tests was not established. It is not described as a full replication.
- This is an interpretive correction to the label's wording only. It changes neither the condition, nor the observed row, nor any analysis.

The closing paragraph of RUNLOG.md (2026-11-03) first said that only the condition had been fixed. That was wrong, because the labels were fixed as well. RUNLOG.md was corrected on 2026-11-03 to match this record.

Where it appears: Manuscript v11 (Results §2, Methods "Independent mouse dataset") and Supplementary v12 (Note 9, "The label of row B").
