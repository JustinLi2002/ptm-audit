## Language model embeddings

`notebooks/protein_features_esm.npy` (16,336 × 1280) and `protein_ids_esm.json`
are frozen mean-pooled ESM-2 650M representations (`esm2_t33_650M_UR50D`,
fair-esm 2.0.0), generated by `extract_esm.py` from `pdisjoint/proteins.fasta`.
Sequences longer than the 1022-residue context were embedded in overlapping
1022-residue windows with 256-residue overlap and pooled with weights
proportional to residue count; 2,223 of 16,336 sequences required this.

Raw mean pairwise cosine similarity is 0.869, reflecting the anisotropy of
transformer representations rather than degeneracy: after subtracting the mean
vector it is 0.001, and the effective rank is 200.5 of 1280. The features are
used uncentred.
