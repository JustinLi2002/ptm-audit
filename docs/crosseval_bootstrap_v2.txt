Corrected cross-evaluation bootstrap, 2026-11-02 (answers the review of the same day; no retraining)

Problem. analysis/crosseval_bootstrap.py (repo v1.5) resampled each split separately, concatenated the three
resampled splits and scored them once. Table 1 reports a different statistic: the mean over the three splits of
the per-split DeltaAUROC. Example: methylation K/R, ProtT5, TT: split mean +0.0716, pooled +0.0372.

Fix. crosseval_bootstrap_v2.py computes the reported statistic in every resample:
  1. whole proteins are drawn with replacement from the union of the test proteins of the three splits; a protein
     in the test partition of more than one split (75-323 of 808-3,593 proteins, by task and test construction)
     carries the same multiplicity in each;
  2. within each split, sites are weighted by their protein's multiplicity and the sequence-only and augmented
     models are scored on the same weighted sites (weighted Mann-Whitney AUROC, checked against sklearn);
  3. the per-split differences are averaged.
2,000 resamples; percentile interval. One stream of draws per (task, training construction, test construction),
seeded from the cell identity and shared by the three features.

Run (about 5 min of scoring on a laptop, plus file loading; inputs are the deposited predictions, Zenodo 21670043, pdisjoint_runs_v2):
  python -X utf8 -E -P crosseval_bootstrap_v2.py <pdisjoint_runs_v2> crosseval_ci_v2.tsv -B 2000 > crosseval_ci_v2.log

Outputs. crosseval_ci_v2.tsv: 96 rows (8 tasks x 4 cells x 3 features); d_auroc is the split mean and agrees with
results/crosseval_verify.tsv to 4e-5; auroc_lo/auroc_hi are the interval; d_split0-2 the per-split values.
